The problem

Saved is not the same as findable

A knowledge worker, founder, investor or researcher with hundreds to thousands of saved URLs and little folder hygiene can't find a useful piece, tell a paper from a podcast, or see which news has expired — without opening every link.

01

No sense of why

"New folder (3)" tells you nothing about relevance, format, or whether the page still says what it said when you saved it.

02

No sense of what's stale

A pricing analysis from two years ago and a reference doc from five years ago look identical in a folder view.

03

No path to a decision

This is a personal library and triage tool, not a social bookmarking feed — the point is a deliberate keep, read-later, or delete call.

First-run journey

From export to a reviewed library

Five steps, and the owner is never more than one undo away from any decision.

01

See the output before you sign up

The landing page explains the output and privacy in one screen. No account is required to start — Guest mode works immediately.

02

Sign in with Google, then import honestly

You land in a private account and choose one of two routes: upload a Chrome bookmark-manager HTML export now, or install the companion Chrome extension for a full import that then stays in sync.

03

Watch it work, or leave and come back

Folders and URLs parse immediately into a raw library while enrichment runs in the background — parsed, fetched, classified, summarized, failed — all visible, none blocking.

04

Search, filter, and triage

Topic groups, searchable items, filters, and a Suggested review queue. Mark Keep, Read later, or Delete candidate — nothing changes in Chrome itself.

05

Export what you decided to keep

Download the full enriched library as CSV/XLSX, or an HTML bookmarks file of the items marked Keep or Read later.

Accounts & sync

Two honest ways in — no third route

Sign in with Google (OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect) uses only openid, email and profile scopes — identity, nothing else. Bookmarks themselves arrive through exactly one of two routes.

Available today

HTML export upload

Export from Chrome's bookmark manager and upload the file. The always-available path — the only one needed at MVP.

  • Re-uploads merge by canonical URL — cheap and expected
  • Every prior triage decision and edit is preserved
  • A diff summary shows what's added, changed, or gone
Fast follow

Companion Chrome extension

Reads your bookmark tree locally via chrome.bookmarks and pushes deltas as you add, edit or remove — the ongoing-sync mechanism.

  • Requests only the bookmarks permission — no history, no tabs, no page content
  • Strictly read-only against Chrome — nothing is edited or deleted in your browser
  • Converges with HTML re-imports on the same canonical records

What Google sign-in cannot do

Google offers no API for a website or third party to read Chrome-synced bookmarks from a Google account — Chrome Sync is first-party only, and Google has stated it won't whitelist third-party token access. The old Google Bookmarks service also shut down on 30 September 2021. So this product never promises "sign in and your Chrome bookmarks appear." Bookmarks arrive only via an HTML export upload or the companion extension, read locally with your consent.

Inside the library

Built for triage, not for browsing

Every surface is designed around one question: keep, read later, or delete — with an explanation, never a silent guess.

Search, filter, sort

Normalized title, domain, URL, folder path, topic and summary — multi-select filters combine with AND across dimensions, OR within one.

Topic clusters

Named from actual bookmark content, not folder names. Rename, merge, or move items later — "Unclassified" is a valid topic, not a failure.

Explainable freshness

"Likely old news," "Review for relevance," "Evergreen/unknown," or "Recent" — each with a reason and a confidence, never a guess dressed as fact.

Reversible triage

Unreviewed, Keep, Read later, or Delete candidate. Every change saves immediately and undoes just as easily — nothing touches Chrome.

Content types that mean something

Article, Document/PDF, Long podcast, Video, or Other/Unknown — classified from MIME, structured data and URL signals.

Export what matters

CSV/XLSX of the full enriched library, or an HTML bookmarks file of the items you chose to keep or read later.

Privacy & safety

Private by default

Account-scoped, encrypted

HTTPS in transit, encrypted storage, and server-side isolation of processing workers. Bookmark URLs never appear in analytics or error logs.

Nothing shared by default

No search-engine indexing of private libraries, no share links unless you opt in — and any future sharing is scoped and previewed first.

A clear disclosure, every time

Before upload, you see exactly what happens: links may be visited by the service and their content sent to the selected AI processor. Delete an import, or your account, whenever you choose.
